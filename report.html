Matrix-Free LVPP: Measured Findings

Session report, 2026-09-03 → 09-10. All numbers measured on the sphere obstacle benchmark (direct LU references verified at every level); harness scripts under lvpp/experiments/; full lab notebook in RESEARCH.md. Where a headline number was used, it was reproduced independently before being recorded.

1. The system and its block structure

LVPP reparameterizes the obstacle constraint \(g = u - \psi_{\mathrm{obs}} \ge 0\) as \(g = e^{\phi}\) with a latent field \(\phi \in \mathbb{R}\) (unconstrained), then regularizes via Bregman proximal point with parameter \(\alpha_k\). One Newton linearization at a deep proximal state is the \(2\times 2\) block system

$$ J \;=\; \begin{bmatrix} K & B \\ B^{\top} & D \end{bmatrix}, \qquad \begin{aligned} K &= \alpha\,(\text{stiffness}) &&\text{(SPD, scales with } \alpha\text{)}\\ B &= M \quad\text{(mass matrix)} &&\\ D &= -\,M \odot e^{\phi} \quad &&\text{(scaled mass — the degenerate block)} \end{bmatrix} $$

Nothing here is symmetric-positive-definite overall, but every block has known character: \(K\) is elliptic-like (healthy), \(B\) is plain mass (healthy), and the entire degeneracy is localized in \(D\): on the contact interior \(e^{\phi}\) underflows (\(\phi \to -318\ldots-763\)) and diagonal entries collapse by 60+ orders of magnitude.

2. The Schur complement is the load-bearing object

Eliminating the (healthy) primal block leaves the latent Schur complement

$$ S \;=\; D - B^{\top} K^{-1} B . $$

The decisive measured fact of this project: S never collapses, even at full degeneracy. On the dead subspace \(D \to 0\), but the coupling term \(-B^{\top}K^{-1}B\) carries those rows exactly because \(B = M\) is a plain mass matrix — its entries do not scale with \(e^{\phi}\). Measured spectrum of \(S\): strictly negative definite, \(\lambda_{S,\min} \approx -5.6\cdot10^{-2} / -1.4\cdot10^{-2} / -3.5\cdot10^{-3}\) across levels — pure \(h^{2}\) scaling, α-independent, no near-null cluster at any depth.

3. The refutation ledger

Four hypotheses were tested and refuted by direct measurement (each with a bit-identical control proving the implementation was active):

  1. Schur-approximation quality — exact per-cell broken-basis Sp changes nothing: coupling |C0| ~ 7.3×10⁻⁴ × |D_floor|; BᵀK̂⁻¹B is O(h⁴) vs D's O(h²), structurally subdominant (hierarchical-pG's recipe targets divergence-coupled systems where the scalings are equal).
  2. Naive active-set rows — identity-patching dead rows destroys the Schur balance: 825 its at L0, diverges at L2. Dead rows must be treated consistently with the coupling.
  3. Rank-k dead-subspace correction — exact on span(V) to 10⁻¹⁷, yet fails at depth: the floored PC's deficiency E − I has slowly-decaying singular values; the band above σ = 0.1 has rank ≥ 70/241/829 ≫ dead count 45/177/765, σ_k still O(1). The floor damages a whole transition band, not just the dead rows — not low-rank-curable.
  4. diag(K)⁻¹ grading mismatch — κ(diag(K)⁻¹K) grows only 3.3e3→8.9e3 across h-ratios 1×→45.6× (predicted ~2000×); ‖Sₚ−S‖ stays ~1.6e-3. Grading-aware diagonal variants are worse than baseline; per-cell coupling stays a negative on graded meshes too.

4. The floor: necessary once, deleted twice

The ψ_floor (ε·mass on the latent block of Jp only) was introduced when the additive config's latent diagonal solve divided by machine-zero rows. It is our addition — the papers' only precedent is a J-level perturbation in the fracture example (ι = 10⁻³), on a non-potential problem. Its measured biography:

  1. Load-bearing for the additive config — without it, PC setup divides by 10⁻⁷⁰ → Inf/NaN → DIVERGED_PC_FAILED. Design rule established by measurement: Jp-only, never on J (operator floor fails at every level; the proximal iteration needs exact Newton steps).
  2. Costly at depth on Schur — the exact-PC decomposition isolated it: unfloored exact-S PC gives outer its = 1 flat at every depth; the same PC with floored S reproduces the entire growth curve (145/895/1000-diverged).
  3. Deleted on the Schur path — Sp = D − M diag(K)⁻¹Mᵀ is nonsingular without any floor (dead rows carried by the coupling); ψ_floor = 0 end-to-end is LU-identical with max its 14/29/48 (was 45/130–560/400–926). The ε-ladder maps the safety band: ε ≤ 1e-5 safe everywhere, 1e-4 the knife edge (non-monotone resonance), ≥ 1e-3 kills L2 cold solves. Drift-concentrated floors land in the bad band and are rejected.

6. Graded meshes: the fix

Free-boundary-refined (band) meshes are dead-row factories — sharpening the boundary converts smeared compromise nodes into cleanly-pinned contact nodes; the dead fraction of latent dofs explodes 7% → 50% (uniform holds ~9%), and outer-GMRES blowup tracks grading strength (h_max/h_min)². The refuted diag(K)⁻¹ mismatch hypothesis (κ grows only 3.3e3→8.9e3 across 1×→45.6×) is recorded alongside. Generality note: CG is valid here because −S is SPD in the symmetric/energy case; for non-potential LVPP systems (convection, q-Laplacian) S becomes nonsymmetric/indefinite and the inner solver must become GMRES/MINRES — the triple-product matvec itself assumes no symmetry, so the architecture transfers with the inner method's name changed.

7. What LVPP promised vs. what it costs

The papers' promises are all delivered in our measurements: no operator mutation ever (bit-exact matfree action through the full proximal loop), smooth well-posed subproblems (prox 8/11/8, Newton 21/23/19 — mesh-independent, LU-identical), feasibility by construction. What was never promised — and is nowhere analyzed in [LVPP] or the pG paper — is a uniformly conditioned linear system: the same smooth change of variables that buys constraint satisfaction makes the latent variable unidentifiable on the contact set, and the spectrum pays. That bill was never itemized before the spectral anatomy; itemizing it is this project's contribution.

8. The final recipe

Production (symmetric/energy case):
  matfree Amat  +  assembled unfloored Pmat (psi_floor = 0)
  +  PCFieldSplit(schur, upper, use_amat=False, selfp)
  +  fieldsplit_0: preonly + MUMPS-LU        (exact K; GAMG is a measured wall-loser)
  +  fieldsplit_1: preonly + MUMPS-LU        (LU of selfp Sp)
  +  outer GMRES rtol 1e-6, restart 250

Graded meshes (h_max/h_min > ~5): replace the Schur block solve with
  matfree S action (triple product) + capped inner CG on −S (rtol 1e-4, cap 40);
  for non-potential families: inner GMRES on S, same discipline.

On adaptive (band) meshes: expect dead-row factories at the refined
boundary ring; uniform meshes minimize solver cost at matched dofs.

10b. True-matfree hpG (final experiment)

The last global factorization (cached Cholesky of A) has been removed from the solve path: K⁻¹ is now a capped CG solve with an AMG hierarchy built once per mesh (preconditioner-only), the Schur block is the matfree triple product, Ŝ stays batched cellwise Cholesky, E_β = 0 in the operator (the §4 decoupling, now measured), capped inner GMRES, outer FGMRES.

Correctness: bit-consistent with serial-direct at p=2 (8/22, err identical). The fair high-order comparison (L0, p=4, both arms, same capped config): iteration counts are identical (8/30, err 9.542e-5, outer 27, inner 39.4/apply) — making A matfree costs a per-apply A-solve premium (3.5× wall at p=4, 1.3× at p=2) that grows with κ(A).

Graded 51.4× p=4 (37k+20k dofs): the assembled arm HOLDS (8/22, err 1.568e-5, 535 s) — the E_β-decoupled + capped-GMRES config stands at high p + strong grading. The matfree arm recorded two honest negatives: GAMG stalls on graded K₀ (1994/1994 non-converged A-CG solves); hypre BoomerAMG is healthy per-iteration (28.7 its avg, zero nonconv) but projected past the session budget. The open preconditioner question is an AMG for graded high-order K; everything else in the upgrade blueprint is now measured.

Remaining open items: an AMG for graded high-order K (GAMG fails, BoomerAMG expensive), native-PETSc reimplementation of the variant-B PC, and the N^0.8 uniform-mesh growth under variant B.
